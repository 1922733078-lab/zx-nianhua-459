R1 Judge by dominant visual technique (brushwork, color treatment, composition), not by
    subject matter alone.
R2 Agitated distortion with emotional intensity = Expressionism; calm naturalism of ordinary
    life = Realism.
R3 Chiaroscuro drama with grand-manner seriousness = Baroque; dreamlike allegorical or
    mystical suggestion = Symbolism.
R4 Broken or simultaneous geometric planes = Cubism (either phase).
R5 Meticulous crisp detail with luminous glazes and hidden domestic symbolism = Northern
    Renaissance.
R6 Light-focused outdoor scenes with small visible strokes = Impressionism; bolder subjective
    color and form going beyond light effects = Post-Impressionism.
