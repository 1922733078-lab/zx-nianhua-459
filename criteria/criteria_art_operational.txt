Operating procedure (apply in order):
P1 Judge brushwork and color treatment FIRST; subject matter LAST.
P2 If small, visible strokes dissolve form in flickering natural light -> Impressionism.
P3 If bold non-naturalistic color, distorted outline and agitated thick stroke -> Expressionism.
P4 If form breaks into geometric planes or shows simultaneous viewpoints -> Cubism.
P5 If plain unidealized rendering of common people, labor or daily interiors -> Realism.
P6 If strong directional light-dark contrast drives a theatrical scene -> Baroque.
P7 If meticulous crisp detail with disguised symbolic objects -> Northern Renaissance.
P8 If a staged dream, vision or allegory overrides realistic rendering -> Symbolism.
P9 If wild sublime nature, storm, ruin or an awe-struck solitary figure dominates -> Romanticism.
P10 If flat whiplash curves and floral ornament organize the whole surface -> Art Nouveau.
P11 If two labels tie, choose the one supported by TECHNIQUE (P2-P10), not by theme.
